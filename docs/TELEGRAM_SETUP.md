# 🤖 Telegram Bot Configuration Guide

RyzenDesk includes a native, two-way Telegram support bot enabling instant alerts for staff and clients without external webhook tunnels.

---

## 🛠️ Step 1: Create Your Bot

1. Open Telegram and message [@BotFather](https://t.me/BotFather).
2. Send `/newbot` and follow the prompts to choose a bot name and unique username (e.g. `RyzenDeskBot`).
3. Copy the HTTP API token generated by BotFather (looks like `7123456789:ABCdefGHIjklMNOpqrsTUVwxyz`).

---

## ⚙️ Step 2: Configure Environment

Add the token to your `.env` file:
```env
TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN="7123456789:ABCdefGHIjklMNOpqrsTUVwxyz"
```

Restart RyzenDesk. The server will automatically initialize long-polling mode — **no public HTTPS webhook URL is required**.

---

## 📱 Bot Commands Reference

### For Support Staff:
- `/staff <password>`: Links your Telegram account to your staff profile to receive immediate ticket notifications and escalation pings.
- `/tickets`: View the top 5 urgent tickets awaiting response.
- `/unlink`: Disconnects Telegram notifications from your profile.

### For Customers / Clients:
- `/link <zt_token>`: Links your Telegram chat to your customer ticket history. You will receive immediate alerts whenever an agent replies to your ticket.
- `/id`: Prints your unique Telegram Chat ID.
